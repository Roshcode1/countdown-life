AGE IN MOTION — TRANSFER PACK (phone -> new laptop)
==================================================
1. Copy this whole AgeTransfer folder to the new laptop.
2. Open: muse-spark-wid\everyting_need.md  (exact file name, spelling as-is).
3. Do step 1 to step 5 in that file. Build time is about 30 minutes.
4. AgeInMotion.apk = latest installable app (also rebuilt by step 4).
